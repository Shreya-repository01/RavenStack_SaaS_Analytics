# RavenStack SaaS Revenue & Subscription Analytics

Team project (Data Analytics Project 2): a validated view of recurring revenue, subscription movements, churn and retention for a SaaS company, with a Power BI dashboard, a report and a presentation.

**Dataset:** *RavenStack: Synthetic SaaS Dataset (Multi-Table)* by **River @ Rivalytics** (fully synthetic, no personal data, MIT-like licence, credit required). Coverage: January 2023 to December 2024.

## Key results

| Measure | June 2024 | December 2024 |
|---|---:|---:|
| MRR | 3,833,405 | 10,159,608 |
| New MRR | 537,758 | 2,273,427 |
| Churned MRR | 20,602 | 574,643 |
| Customer churn rate | 9.48% | 15.94% |
| Revenue churn rate | 0.62% | 6.79% |

- The MRR bridge (opening + new - churned = closing) is exactly 0 in all 24 months.
- Cohort retention falls from about 91% in the signup month to about 44% after 12 months.
- CAC is **not** calculated (the data has no acquisition spend). LTV is shown only as an illustrative, revenue-only estimate.
- All findings are associations in a synthetic dataset, not proven causes.

```bash

cd RavenStack_SaaS_Analytics
python -m venv .venv
.venv\Scripts\activate          # Mac/Linux: source .venv/bin/activate
```

## Metric definitions (summary)

Full rules: `docs/metric_definitions.md`.

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Active subscription | `start_date` on or before the date and `end_date` empty or after it |
| MRR | Sum of `mrr_amount` of active, non-trial subscriptions at month-end (already monthly, never divide annual plans) |
| New MRR / Churned MRR | `mrr_amount` of paid subscriptions that start / end in the month |
| Customer churn rate | Distinct accounts signed up before the month with a non-reactivation churn event in the month, divided by those accounts |
| Revenue churn rate | Churned MRR divided by opening MRR |
| Cohort retention | Cohort = signup month; retained = no non-reactivation churn event yet |
| LTV (illustrative) | ARPA divided by 12-month average monthly customer churn; revenue only |

**Dashboard measure dictionary :**  

Measure	Plain-words definition
MRR	Sum of mrr_amount of active paid subscriptions at the month-end
MRR Opening	MRR at the last day of the previous month
New MRR	mrr_amount of paid subscriptions that started in the month
Churned MRR	mrr_amount of paid subscriptions that ended in the month
Bridge Check	Opening + New − Churned − MRR (must be 0)
Revenue Churn Rate %	Churned MRR ÷ MRR Opening × 100
Opening Customers	Accounts that signed up before the month starts
Churned Customers	Distinct opening customers with a non-reactivation churn event in the month
Customer Churn Rate %	Churned Customers ÷ Opening Customers × 100
Active Customers	Accounts with at least one active paid subscription at the month-end
ARPA	MRR ÷ Active Customers
Avg Monthly Churn 12M %	Average monthly Customer Churn Rate % over the last 12 months
LTV (Illustrative Est.)	ARPA ÷ (Avg Monthly Churn 12M % ÷ 100); revenue only, no margin
Cohort Size	Accounts in the signup-month cohort
Cohort Retained	Cohort accounts with no non-reactivation churn event on or before month N
Cohort Retention %	Cohort Retained ÷ Cohort Size × 100
Expansion MRR (info only)	New paid subscriptions flagged upgrade; not part of the bridge
## Known limitations

- Synthetic data: findings describe this dataset, not a real company.
- No acquisition spend, discounts, taxes, currency or payment events. CAC is not supported.
- Expansion and contraction MRR are not reported as definitive KPIs (only upgrade and downgrade flags exist, not amounts).
- Accounts hold about 10 overlapping subscriptions and about 90% of subscriptions never end, so MRR rises almost every month.
- Three churn signals disagree (110 flagged accounts, 352 accounts with churn events, 486 ended subscriptions); customer churn uses churn events.
- 152 subscriptions start and end in the same month and are invisible in month-end counts.
- 19,142 usage records are dated before their subscription start.
- Early 2023 months have very small bases, so their rates are volatile.
